Events

Things on in the town at set times (weiss.world_events). Every rule given must hold, and an empty one means any: no weekdays is every day, no times is all day. Dates are a save's calendar (every save starts on Monday 18 November). A sale's settings: {"what": "packs", "discount": 50, "featured": true, "limit": 5} (what is packs, specials or cards; cards can add "extra": 2 rares). A tournament's: {"fee": 500, "prizes": {"champion": 3000, "final": 1500, "semi": 600}, "champion_pack": "lucky_pack", "points": 8}, on Saturday (entry) and Sunday (the rounds).
