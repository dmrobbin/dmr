About models and aggression

Aggression 1 is the standard behaviour. Higher values chase enemy characters harder and protect their own less.

Focused works towards its climax combos, saving cards and stock for the next one it can reach. Strategic gets the most cards it can before building its board, filling its waiting room first if it can take cards from there.
