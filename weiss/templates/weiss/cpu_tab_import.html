1. Download the export

Every CPU, scene and playable anchor as it is now, for writing new CPUs from (the new-cpu skill reads it). Unzip it over corpus/: in PowerShell, Expand-Archive -Force $HOME\Downloads\corpus.zip corpus. Download it again after importing, so the next CPUs see the names, scenes and anchors just taken.

Download export (.zip)

2. Check and import CPU files

Choose one or more files from corpus/new/. They're checked first, nothing saved, decks built as they will be. They go in together, whole or not at all, so their scenes can cast each other. None is finalized.
